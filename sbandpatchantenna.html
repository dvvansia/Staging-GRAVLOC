Overview

The S-Band Patch Antenna is designed for critical telemetry, tracking, and command (TT&C) applications. It offers exceptional performance in a low-profile form factor, ensuring minimal drag and weight impact on spacecraft and aerospace vehicles.

  • High orbital reliability with rigorous thermal vacuum testing.
  • Precision engineered for minimal signal loss and interference.
  • Compatible with standard aerospace mounting interfaces.
  • ITAR compliant manufacturing processes.

Technical Properties

Frequency Band S-Band (2.0 – 2.5 GHz)
