Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the GRAVLOC Supplier Program? expand_more

The program is open to qualified manufacturers and distributors of space-grade components, materials, and services. All suppliers undergo a vetting process to ensure quality and compliance standards for the aerospace industry are met.

Is there a fee to list my products? expand_more

Basic listing is currently free for vetted suppliers. We offer premium tiers for enhanced visibility, advanced analytics, and priority placement in search results.

What technical data do I need to provide? expand_more

We recommend providing comprehensive datasheets, CAD models (STEP/IGES), heritage data (if applicable), compliance certifications, and clear environmental tolerances to maximize engineer engagement.

How do I get started with the onboarding process? expand_more

Simply click "Join Now" or "Get Started" to begin the registration process. You'll create a company profile, provide your product catalogue details, and our team will review and activate your supplier account. The entire process typically takes less than 30 minutes.

What kind of support does GRAVLOC provide to suppliers? expand_more

We provide dedicated onboarding support, ongoing account management, and technical assistance to help you optimise your product listings. You'll also have access to market insights, performance analytics, and priority support through our supplier dashboard.
